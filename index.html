About

The person behind the books.

I'm Adewumi Ayomide, a Nigerian-based editor, formatter, cover designer, and KDP publisher. I help indie authors publish books that feel like they came out of a major house.

A

I started in publishing the way most of us do, obsessed with books, frustrated that so many good ones were let down by their covers, interiors, or introductions. Five years and 180+ titles later, I've built a small practice around one idea: a great book deserves great craft on every page.

Today I work with fantasy, romance, thriller, and non-fiction authors. Some come with a raw draft. Some come with a finished manuscript and no idea how to publish it. Most come with a mix of ambition and uncertainty, and that's exactly where I'm most useful.

I care about voice. I care about typography. I care about the moment a reader picks up a book, opens the first page, and forgets to make dinner. If that's the kind of book you want to make, we'll get along.

Service · Editing

Editing that keeps your voice, sharpens the rest.

Four editing stages, each priced at a flat $0.006 per word. Pick one, pick two, or run the full sequence.

$0.006 / word · per stage

Most editors bundle passes or hide the real cost. I price each stage separately so you only pay for what your manuscript actually needs.

Every edit begins with a full read. I'll come back with a manuscript evaluation: what's working, what isn't, and which stages will give you the cleanest result.

The four stages

  • Developmental editing — plot, pacing, character arcs, story architecture.
  • Line editing — clarity, rhythm, tone, and flow.
  • Copyediting — grammar, syntax, consistency, style-sheet alignment.
  • Proofreading — final polish for typos and formatting glitches.

Process & Booking

The way I actually work.

No mystery, no scope creep. Here's the exact sequence every author I work with moves through, from the first email to the launch.

01

Proposal & timeline

You get a written proposal within 2 hours: scope, price, milestones, and a start date.

02

Project kick-off

Once you approve the proposal, I open a shared workspace and lock in the schedule so nothing gets lost in email.

03

The work

For editing, I read the whole manuscript first. For design, I deliver exactly the vision you brief me on. For publishing, we build a launch calendar.

04

Your review

One or two review rounds are baked in. Small changes are free. Big pivots go through a change order so we both stay honest.

05

Delivery

Final files, source files, style sheet, and a launch checklist, all polished to the highest quality so your book is ready to launch.
